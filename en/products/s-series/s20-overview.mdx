## Product overview

S20 is an industrial near-panoramic RGB-D camera designed for indoor and outdoor 3D perception. Based on dToF technology, it outputs depth maps, IR images, point clouds, and RGB images, with RGB and depth data aligned spatially and temporally inside the camera.

The device integrates a 200 Hz IMU and SoC, supports software triggering, and works with SLAM algorithms such as the FastLio family for mapping, localization, and environmental perception. In addition to the standard camera, S20 is available as a smaller custom MIPI module.

## Integration and deployment

<Warning>
  S20 has a 170° horizontal field of view. Mounting structures, protective windows, bezels, and cables must not obstruct the field of view. If a protective window is required, verify its transmittance at 940 nm and its effect on ranging accuracy.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plan the field of view and mounting space">
    Reserve clearance based on the 170° × 68° ± 3° ToF field of view, and keep mirrors, bright metal surfaces, and narrow reflective cavities out of the near-field view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the interface and power architecture">
    The standard camera uses Ethernet and 12–27 VDC power. For space-constrained devices, evaluate the custom MIPI module and complete the mechanical and electrical design according to its delivery documentation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure synchronization and data links">
    Configure PTP, the 200 Hz IMU, and software triggering for the system architecture, and confirm stable reception of depth, IR, point-cloud, and RGB data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Validate the target environment">
    Validate operating range, accuracy, RGB-D alignment, and SLAM stability under the target reflectivity, ambient light, temperature, vibration, and motion-speed conditions.
  </Step>
</Steps>
